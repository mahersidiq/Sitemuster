# 08 — English and Spanish internationalization at launch

## Exact stack and locale behavior

`expo-localization` detects suggested device locale; `i18next` and `react-i18next` render all user-facing strings. Supported locales **en-US** and **es-US**. First launch asks the worker/staff user to choose EN or ES instead of silently forcing device choice. Save language in profile and encrypted/local preference as needed. Language toggle in Profile/Settings at any time. Missing translation is a failing CI test; fallback may show English ONLY on development builds with visible localization warning, never silently ship partly untranslated V1 screens.

## Externalize everything

No hardcoded English inside React components, validation messages, native permission rationale, kiosk mode, incoming invitation text, OTP instructions, QR session screens, role-specific menus, consent notices, app notifications, SMS templates where controlled, daily report headings, worker CSV column labels or Muster Mode. Use key dictionaries and ICU-style interpolation/pluralization as applicable. Name/company/address remain as entered; do not machine-translate names.

## Day-one bilingual flows

- GC project creation and site configuration.
- Safety orientation, status ORIENTATION_COMPLETED and separate approval / employer verification.
- Worker SMS login, resends, rate limits, error messages, alternative for no phone.
- GPS permission rationale, difference of foreground/Always, precise, offsite monitoring.
- Kiosk worker dynamic/printed badges, visitors, offline sync and worker confirmation.
- Foreman crew check-in, disputed daily reports, deadline.
- Worker hours CSV and correction request.
- Muster push, I'M SAFE, physically verified, unaccounted/unknown, emergency roster.
- Account deletion/privacy and appeal/error messages.

## Consent and legal content

UI translation is separate from legal notice review. `docs/legal/TRANSLATION_REVIEW.md` holds document IDs, language, hash, reviewer and status. Draft ES legal content from AI may appear in staging with DRAFT watermark but cannot be published for production consent until a human bilingual legal reviewer changes its status to APPROVED. Same rule for substantive EN policy legal approval. Backend denies submission against unapproved versions. Consent records include locale, version, scope, signed/acknowledged method, accepted time and worker ID. A GC customization produces new notice version, translation review and renewed consent where legally required.

## Formatting

Use `Intl` and the project's actual IANA timezone to display shift/day, not a fixed -0600 offset. US English vs US Spanish terms: minutes/hours, decimal handling, AM/PM readable; preserve server UTC. Provide accessible labels/announcements in the selected language. Screen widths must support longer Spanish labels; never truncate safety actions or important GPS statuses.

## Verification

Automated test compares locale keysets exactly (including placeholders and plural categories), scans code for hardcoded copy, screenshots major worker and GC flows in both languages, and verifies notification templates and CSV labels. Test 100-worker orientation in Spanish. Manual Spanish comprehension test required before pilot.
